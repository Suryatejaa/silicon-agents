What this page proves
It shows that the product is being used, that reviewers are accepting or rejecting actions, that parser trust is visible, and that results are leaving the product through workflow exports instead of staying trapped in a demo UI.
How to use this in a pilot review
Start with total runs and acceptance rate, move to parser confidence and warning patterns, then open recent runs in Run History to show raw artifacts, decisions, exports, and feedback loops end to end.
